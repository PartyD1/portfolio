## Executive summary

- Audit Health Score: **13/20** (Acceptable).
- Issues: **P0 0, P1 3, P2 10, P3 6**.
- The three things that matter most:
  1. **Dark theme legibility over the blobs.** On phones the blobs are 76 to 84vw wide and the wash is fixed, so a large share of every screen is lit blob. ### P1

**[P1-1] Dark theme: text over the lit blobs fails 4.5:1 across the site**
- Location: `app/globals.css` tokens `.dark { --glass: 0.07; --glass-strong: 0.11; --glass-raised: 0.16; .wash__blob opacity 0.62 }`; affected runs are `.card__tagline`, `.card__repo`, `.card__use`, `.pill`, `.timeline__title`, `.timeline__ownership`, `.timeline__company`, `.timeline__period`, `.flow__title`, `.flow__detail`, `.flow__bus-title`, `.case__points li`, `.case__back`, `.case__media__cap`, `.case__foot-lede`, `.about__body p`, `.status`, `.footer`, plus display text (`h1.case__title`, `h2.section__title`, `.card__name`, `.hero__name`) under the 3:1 large-text floor.
- Category: Accessibility (and Theming).
- Measured (390 wide, dark): card taglines p20 1.8 to 2.5; timeline title 1.8, ownership 1.9; flow details 1.9 to 2.0; case bullets 2.1 to 2.3; back link 2.2; captions 2.1 to 2.4; footer 2.3; `h1.case__title` 2.4 to 2.6 (needs 3.0); card names 2.9 (needs 3.0). At 1440 wide dark: timeline title 2.0, status pill 2.1, footer 2.3, taglines 2.3 to 2.7, timeline company 2.7. The median in most of these runs is 6 to 10, which is the point: the same paragraph is fine over indigo and unreadable over the blob.
- Impact: in dark mode on a phone, roughly a fifth to a third of every body run sits on a mint, yellow or pink blob at about 2:1. Low-vision readers lose sentences mid-paragraph.
- WCAG: 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum), AA. Fails.
- Recommendation: the fix has to change what is behind the text, not the ink (the ink is already at `#cbcae2` / `#ecebf4`). Options, in order of preference: (a) make the dark glass ground-tinted rather than white-tinted, e.g. `--glass: color-mix(in oklab, var(--ground) 72%, transparent)` and `--glass-strong` / `--glass-raised` heavier still, so a card reads as indigo over the blob instead of a lit blob through a veil; (b) drop `.dark .wash__blob` opacity to about 0.4 under 720px, where the blobs are largest relative to content; (c) give the footer, the About body and the status line a surface (see P2-4 and P3-17). Re-measure with the pixel method after any change; the light theme sits at the edge too (P2-13), so tune both.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

**[P1-2] Single-character keyboard shortcuts with no modifier and no off switch**
- Location: `components/Shell.tsx`, the `keydown` listener on `window` (keys `1` to `4`, only inputs and contenteditable are excluded).
- Category: Accessibility.
- Measured: pressing `3` on `/work/wordplay` with nothing focused navigated to `/#about`.
- Impact: speech-input users and anyone who types a digit while not in a field are navigated away; screen readers in browse mode pass digits through. There is no setting to disable or remap.
- WCAG: 2.1.4 Character Key Shortcuts, Level A. Fails.
- Recommendation: keep the keycaps in the menu and make the shortcuts live only while the sheet is open (that is where the keycaps are shown anyway, and it satisfies the "active only when a component has focus" exception). If they should stay global, require a modifier (`Alt+1`) or add a persisted toggle in the menu foot.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

**[P1-3] Experience timeline renders `<div>` children directly inside `<ol>`**
- Location: `components/Experience.tsx` (`<ol className="timeline__list">` wrapping `<Reveal className="timeline__item">`, and `Reveal` renders a `div`).
- Category: Accessibility.
- Measured: DOM shows `ol.timeline__list > div.timeline__item` twice on every homepage capture.
- Impact: invalid HTML; assistive tech does not announce the roles as a list of two items, and the ordered sequence (the one signal the section is built around) is lost.

## Patterns and systemic issues

1. **The wash is fixed and the blobs are viewport-sized, so "which text is over a blob" is decided by scroll position, not by layout.** Every contrast failure above is the same failure: text with no surface, or with dark glass that is nearly transparent, at a viewport corner. DESIGN.md already names the rule (Surface-Not-Spacing) and the calm band; the dark tokens do not honour it on phones. Fix the token, not the instances.
2. **rem floors and `nowrap` are the two things that break at 320px and at 200% text.** The hero was deliberately px-bounded for exactly this reason; the section title, the case title, the menu link and the pills were not.
3. **Pressable sizing is consistent at 39 to 43px,** just under the 44px comfort size, everywhere. One `min-height` on the shared link vocabulary fixes all of them at once.
4. **The one shadcn primitive left in the UI (Sheet) carries its defaults through:** the close button, the slide animation under reduced motion, the `sm:max-w-sm` width that the display type cannot fit. Owning those three defaults in `.menu` closes three findings.

## Reproducing the measurements

Build in a worktree (a `next dev` in the main checkout owns `.next` and will clobber a production build there), then `npx next start -p 3111`. The contrast method: screenshot each viewport slice, decode it on a canvas, and for every element with a direct text node sample the pixels one row inside its top and bottom edges at five x positions plus three points just outside each side; discard samples within an RGB distance of 60 from the text colour; report the minimum, 20th percentile and median ratio. `playwright-core` 1.62 with the cached Chromium 1234 headless shell drove it; do not add Playwright to this project's dependencies for it.
